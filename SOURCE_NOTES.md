# Source notes

Obi follows the Judo Vlaanderen **Groeimodel, "leerlijn techniek"**
(<https://judovlaanderengroeimodel.be/leerlijn-techniek/>; per-belt pages published July 2023,
last changed between November 2023 and March 2024). It does not follow the earlier 2015 kyu
programme.

## What we take from the source

- Which techniques each belt requires, and at which stage (basis, verdieping, verpersoonlijking).
- The Japanese names, in the Groeimodel's hyphenated spelling.

## What we write ourselves

- All Dutch and English translations and descriptions. The Groeimodel's Dutch descriptions and
  prose are not reproduced.
- The intro text for the white belt.
- The kanji. They come from standard Kodokan usage, not from the Groeimodel, which doesn't give any.

## Deviations from the source

Every place where Obi knowingly differs from the Groeimodel is listed here, so it can be checked.

| Belt | Source says | Obi does | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oranje (4de kyu) | Lists O-soto-gari under *basis* again, after it was already *basis* at geel | Introduces O-soto-gari at geel only | It can't be new at two belts; the orange list looks like a copy slip. Applied when the orange belt is added. |
